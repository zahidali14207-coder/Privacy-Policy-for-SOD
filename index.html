Privacy Policy - Sim Owner Details

Last Updated: 29-November-2025

Thank you for using Sim Owner Details. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Sim Owner Details does not collect, store, or share any personal information from users.

  • We do not request personal details
  • We do not access contacts, photos, SMS, location, or device information
  • We do not track app usage or monitor user activity

2. Use of Public API

Our app uses a publicly available API to fetch SIM-related information. The API does not require any personal data from the user.

The app only sends the user’s input (e.g., entered number) to the API to retrieve publicly available information. No data is stored, shared, or reused.

3. No Data Storage

The app does not store any information on our servers. We do not create user accounts or retain any data entered in the app.

4. No Third-Party Analytics

Sim Owner Details does not use:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Third-party tracking SDKs

Your activity is never monitored.

5. Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no risk of data leakage from our side. The public API used by the app follows its own security policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated in the app.
