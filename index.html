Privacy Policy - Sim Owner Details

Last Updated: 29-November-2025

Thank you for using Sim Owner Details. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is used in the app in compliance with Google Play policies.

1. Information We Use

Sim Owner Details allows users to enter a phone number in order to retrieve SIM-related information. The entered phone number is required for the app’s core functionality.

This information is processed ephemerally, meaning it is used only at the time of the request and is not stored, saved, or retained by the app or its developer.

2. How the Information Is Used

The phone number entered by the user is sent to a publicly available third-party API solely to fetch the requested SIM information.

  • The phone number is not stored on our servers
  • The phone number is not logged or cached
  • The phone number is not reused for any purpose

3. Data Storage and Retention

Sim Owner Details does not store any personal data. All user input is discarded immediately after the API response is returned.

4. Data Sharing

User-entered phone numbers are shared only with the external API required to provide the lookup service. No data is shared with advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Sim Owner Details does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs, including:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Advertising or tracking services

6. Security

All API requests are made over secure HTTPS connections to protect data during transmission. No personal data is stored by the app.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or store information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
