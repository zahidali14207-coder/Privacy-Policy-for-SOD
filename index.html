Privacy Policy - Sim Owner Details

Last Updated: 20-November-2026

Thank you for using Sim Owner Details. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is used in the app in compliance with Google Play policies.

1. Information We Use

Sim Owner Details allows users to enter a phone number to retrieve SIM-related information. The entered phone number is required for the app's core functionality.

This information is processed temporarily. It is used only at the time of the request and is not saved or retained by the app or its developer.

2. How the Information Is Used

The phone number entered by the user is sent to a publicly available third-party API solely to fetch the requested SIM information.

  • The phone number is not stored on our servers
  • The phone number is not logged or cached
  • The phone number is not reused for any purpose

3. Data Storage and Retention

Sim Owner Details does not store personal data entered in the app. All user input is discarded immediately after the API response is returned.

4. Data Sharing

User-entered phone numbers are shared only with the external API required to provide the lookup service. We also use Google services for analytics and advertising as described below.

5. Analytics and Advertising

We use Google Firebase for analytics and Google AdMob for advertising. These services may collect and process certain information automatically, which may include device identifiers, approximate location, advertising identifiers, app usage data, crash data, and diagnostic information.

The data collected by Firebase and AdMob may be used to provide analytics, measure app performance, deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and improve the app and advertising experience. Google may collect, use, and process this information according to its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Security

API requests are made over secure HTTPS connections. However, information processed by third-party Firebase, AdMob, or API services is handled according to their respective security policies.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or store personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
